'''Yama''' (stylized in [[lowercase]]; {{Langx|ja|ヤマ}}) is a Japanese singer affiliated with [[Sony Music Entertainment Japan]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=yama |url=https://www.sonymusic.co.jp/artist/yama |access-date=2024-06-16 |website=Sony Music Official Site |language=ja}}</ref> After starting a singing career with [[Vocaloid]] covers, they became the vocalist of the [[Rock music|rock]] trio [[BIN (band)|BIN]], and they also have performed music for the [[anime]] ''[[2.43: Seiin High School Boys Volleyball Team]]'', ''[[Ranking of Kings]]'', ''[[Spy × Family (TV series)|Spy × Family]]'',  ''[[Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury]],'' and [[Sanda (manga)|''Sanda'']].

==Biography==
While in junior high school, Yama started making Vocaloid covers at home.<ref>{{Cite interview |last=Yama |interviewer=Kaori Komatsu |script-title=ja:yamaの素顔に迫るスペシャル・インタビュー。新作アルバムへの思いから、自分のことを語り始めると決めた理由まで。 |url=https://www.vogue.co.jp/lifestyle/article/interview-yama |access-date=2024-06-17 |work=Vogue Japan |date=2021-09-02 |language=ja-JP}}</ref> In March 2018, under the name "Yama" (stylized in lowercase), Yama made their singing debut with a YouTube cover of Picon's "Atatakai Ikimono".<ref name=":0">{{Cite news |last=Kuroda |first=Takanori |date=2021-01-07 |script-title=ja:yama、これまで"明かしてこなかったこと"を語るーーシンガーとしての挫折から、クリエイターらとの制作エピソードまで |url=https://realsound.jp/2021/01/post-686164.html |access-date=2024-06-17 |work=Real Sound |page=1-[https://realsound.jp/2021/01/post-686164_2.html 2] |language=ja}}</ref> In 2019, Yama formed the rock trio [[BIN (band)|BIN]], as the group's vocalist under the stage name Yamagami.<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.billboard-japan.com/d_news/detail/88324/2|title=アフター・コロナのヒットパターン?! yamaの「春を告げる」が急上昇|first=Hitoshi|last=Kurimoto|work=Daily News|publisher=Billboard Japan|date=2020-05-23|accessdate=2024-06-17}}</ref><ref>{{Cite news |date=2021-01-26 |script-title=ja:山上、トマト、Tの3人組・BINが1stアルバム「COLONY」発表（動画あり） |url=https://natalie.mu/music/news/413828 |access-date=2024-06-17 |work=Ongaku Natalie |language=ja}}</ref>

In November 2019, Yama participated as a guest vocalist in a special version of {{ill|Kujira (musician)|ja|くじら (作曲家)|lt=Kujira}}'s song "Nemurumachi".<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://realsound.jp/2020/01/post-485034.html|title=くじら「ねむるまち」Spotifyバイラルチャート急上昇 新世代ボカロPの詞曲に共通する"諦念"のムード|work= Real Sound|date=21 January 2020 |accessdate=2024-06-17}}</ref> In April 2020, Yama's first original song, "Haru wo Tsugeru", was released;<ref>{{Cite web |date=2022-08-24 |script-title=ja:yamaのプロフィール |url=https://www.oricon.co.jp/prof/767697/ |access-date=2024-06-17 |website=Oricon News}}</ref> it peaked at #7 on the [[Billboard Japan Hot 100]] and #8 at the [[Oricon Combined Singles Chart]].<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://www.billboard-japan.com/charts/detail?a=hot100&year=2020&month=06&day=8|title=Billboard Japan Hot 100 {{!}} Charts|accessdate=2021-06-27|publisher=Billboard JAPAN}}</ref><ref name="oriconcos"/>

In 2021, Yama sang "Mahi", the opening theme of ''[[2.43: Seiin High School Boys Volleyball Team]]'', and in February 2021, it was released as a single from Sony Music Records.<ref name="oricon 1378076" /> In September 2021, Yama's first album, ''The Meaning of Life'', was released.<ref name="oricon 1403084" /> Yama later sang "Oz", the ending theme of ''[[Ranking of Kings]]'';<ref>{{Cite news |last=Mateo |first=Alex |date=2021-10-01 |title=Ranking of Kings Anime's Promo Video Previews 1st Episode |url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2021-10-01/ranking-of-kings-anime-promo-video-previews-1st-episode/.178062 |access-date=2024-06-18 |work=Anime News Network}}</ref> in November 2021, it was released as part of another single, "Oz/Sekai wa Utsukushī Hazunanda/Smokey Heroine",<ref name="oricon 1428273" /> with all three songs in their 2022 album "Versus the Night".<ref name="oricon 1449447"/>

In 2022, Yama's song "Shikisai", was featured as the ending theme for the second half of ''[[Spy × Family (TV series)|Spy × Family]]''{{'}}s first season;<ref>{{Cite news |last=Loo |first=Egan |date=2022-10-01 |title=Spy×Family Anime's 2nd Half Streams Clean Ending Sequence with yama's Ending Song |url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2022-10-01/spy-family-anime-2nd-half-streams-clean-ending-sequence-with-yama-ending-song/.190412 |access-date=2024-06-18 |work=Anime News Network}}</ref> it was released as a single in November 2022.<ref name="oricon 1457723" /> In 2023, Yama performed "Slash", the opening theme of the second season of ''[[Mobile Suit Gundam: The Witch from Mercury]]'';<ref>{{Cite news |last=Pineda |first=Rafael Antonio |date=2023-03-09 |title=Gundam: The Witch From Mercury Anime Season 2's Trailer Reveals yama's Opening Song |url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2023-03-09/gundam-the-witch-from-mercury-anime-season-2-trailer-reveals-yama-opening-song/.195776 |work=Anime News Network}}</ref> it was later released as a single that year, alongside another album the next year.<ref name="oricon 1473837" /><ref name="oricon 1497789" /> From 28 November to 5 December 2023, Yama held a solo concert tour throughout Southeast Asia and Taiwan, performing in Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Manila, Bangkok, and Taipei.<ref>{{Cite news |last=Pineda |first=Rafael Antonio |date=2023-10-03 |title=Singer yama Goes on Solo Asian Tour in November-December |url=https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2023-10-03/singer-yama-goes-on-solo-asian-tour-in-november-december/.203048 |access-date=2024-06-18 |work=Anime News Network}}</ref>

Yama uses a [[genderfluid]] public image, and their voice has been described by Azrin Tan of ''[[Vogue Singapore]]'' as "soulful, feminine".<ref>{{Cite news |last=Tan |first=Azrin |date=2023-05-23 |title=Japanese artists are going faceless—and they're setting a new standard for the industry |url=https://vogue.sg/faceless-japanese-artists/ |access-date=2024-06-18 |work=Vogue Singapore}}</ref> Most of Yama's personal life is kept anonymous, including their true identity and gender.<ref name=":0"/><ref>{{Cite news |last=Komatsu |first=Mikikazu |date=2022-10-03 |title=Singer yama Shows Off Their First Dance Performance in SPY x FAMILY Cour 2 Ending Theme MV |url=https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/latest/2022/10/3/singer-yama-shows-off-hisher-first-dance-performance-in-spy-x-family-cour-2-ending-theme-mv |access-date=2024-06-18 |work=Crunchyroll News}}</ref> When asked about this, Yama cited a lack of self-confidence and a desire to keep a real-life identity separate from their career.<ref name=":0"/>
